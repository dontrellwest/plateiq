Privacy Policy

PlateIQ · last updated 6 September 2026

PlateIQ does not collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party services, and it makes no network connections of any kind. There is no server to send anything to.

What the app stores, and where

PlateIQ saves your workout history, your settings and your plate rack on your device, using the operating system's own app storage. That information never leaves the device except as part of your own device backup.

  • It is included in an iCloud or Finder backup of your iPhone, so it carries across when you set up a new phone from that backup.
  • It is not readable by us, because it is never sent anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

PlateIQ asks for permission to send you notifications only if you switch on Alert on the lock screen in Settings. That permission is used for one thing: a local notification telling you a rest period has ended. Nothing is sent over the internet, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children

PlateIQ is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, no change here can retroactively affect data already gathered — there is none.
