PlateIQ · a barbell loading calculator

PlateIQ tells you which plates to put on the bar for a target weight, builds a warm-up ladder up to it, times your rests, and keeps a log of what you actually lifted. It works entirely offline.

Getting help

Email dtorry.west@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version, the app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen), and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

The number shown in landmine mode isn't what's on the bar. That's deliberate. A landmine has one end pinned in an anchor, so part of the load goes through the pivot. The app shows the effective weight — what the lift actually feels like.

Will my history survive a new phone? Yes, if you set the new phone up from an iCloud or Finder backup. Your data lives on the device and travels with that backup. There are no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to.

The rest alert didn't reach me. Check that Alert on the lock screen is on in Settings, and that iOS has notifications enabled for PlateIQ in Settings › PlateIQ › Notifications.

Can I add my own exercises? Not yet. PlateIQ ships with nine lifts.

Privacy
