PlateIQ

Built for people who hate arithmetic mid-set.

A barbell loading calculator for iPhone. Give it a target weight and it shows you exactly which plates go on each sleeve, builds the warm-up ladder up to it, times your rests, and logs what you actually lifted. Barbells, loadable dumbbells and landmines, in kilos or pounds, with or without the plates you actually own.

No accounts. No analytics. No network connections. Everything stays on your phone.
